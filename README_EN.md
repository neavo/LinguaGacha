## 📢 Overview
- [LinguaGacha](https://github.com/neavo/LinguaGacha) (/ˈlɪŋɡwə ˈɡɑːtʃə/), a next-generation `AGENT Harness` specialized in translation
- `One-Click`
  - Fully automatic one-click translation across languages
  - Fully automatic translation and review with no manual intervention
- `High Quality`
  - Export freely without restrictions
  - Fine-tuned for each type of content to deliver the best quality
  - Smart terminology management and review help you tailor the wording to your preferences through a simple conversation
- `All Formats, including but not limited to:`
  - Audio & Video - `ASMR`, `Anime`, `TV & Film`, `Motion Comics`
  - Images & Comics - `Manga`, `American comics`, `Black and white`, `Color`, `Horizontal text`, `Vertical text`
  - Subtitles & E-books - `.txt`, `.md`, `.epub`, `.pdf`, `.srt`, `.ass`, `.ssa`, `.vtt`, `.lrc`
  - Games & Data Files - `MTool`, `Godot`, `RenPy`, `Tyrano`, `Kirikiri`, `Galgame`, `WOLFRPG`, `RPGMAKER`, `SRPG Studio`

## 🛸 Basic Workflow
- Download application from [Releases page](https://github.com/neavo/LinguaGacha/releases)
  - Windows:
    - Download `*_Windows_x64.zip` or `*_Windows_arm64.zip` according to your CPU type
    - Extract it and double-click `app.exe` to launch
  - <details>
    <summary>macOS:</summary>
    - Download `*_macOS_x64.dmg` or `*_macOS_arm64.dmg` according to your CPU type
    - Drag it to the Applications folder, but do not launch it yet
    - Open Terminal, enter `sudo xattr -rd com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LinguaGacha.app`, then press Enter
    - Enter your system password, close Terminal, and you can launch it normally
    </details>
  - <details>
    <summary>Linux:</summary>
    - Download `*_Linux_x64.AppImage` or `*_Linux_arm64.AppImage` according to your CPU type
    - Add executable permission with `chmod +x LinguaGacha*.AppImage`
    - Run `./LinguaGacha*.AppImage`
    </details>
- Obtain access to a reliable AI model API, such as [ [DeepSeek](https://github.com/neavo/LinguaGacha/wiki/DeepSeekEN) ]
- Launch the app, create a project, drag your `files to translate` into the `AGENT` input box, and press Enter!

## ❓ FAQ

- Can I translate without `AGENT`\
  For built-in formats such as text files and subtitles, run batch translation in the `Workbench` or use the command line. [See steps](https://github.com/neavo/LinguaGacha/wiki/FAQEN#-can-i-translate-without-agent)

- Can I keep existing translations after a game or file update\
  In the existing project, use `Update files` in `AGENT` to keep completed translations, then translate new or changed content. [See steps](https://github.com/neavo/LinguaGacha/wiki/FAQEN#-can-i-keep-existing-translations-after-a-game-or-file-update)
